2016
The neighbour
git init love-story
2016

A Familiar Face

Our story began in 2016 when I moved into the neighborhood. He was already there, living just a stone's throw from my new home. Sometimes, I would see him sitting in front of his family's house. Among the other men around, something about him made him stand out to me. I couldn't explain it, and I didn't know what it meant. I simply noticed him.

Back then, we were just two neighbors exchanging casual greetings, completely unaware of what the future had in store for us. Little did I know that this familiar face would eventually become my forever.

2017
Friends first
// the first lines of code
2017

The Squeaky Bicycle

By 2017, those casual greetings had blossomed into friendship. He was a teacher then, and every morning, I would recognize the familiar squeak of his bicycle as he rode off to work. It became a little part of my daily routine, an ordinary sound that would eventually become one of my favorite memories.

Our conversations grew, laughter came more easily, and our friendship developed naturally. If our story were a program, these were the first lines of code: simple, unassuming, and quietly laying the foundation for everything that followed.

2018
Something more
git checkout -b relationship
2018

Beyond Friendship

In 2018, our story began moving beyond friendship. We entered a relationship, although it wasn't particularly serious at first. We were still learning about each other and discovering what our connection could become.

But sometimes, love doesn't arrive fully formed; it begins as something small and gradually grows into something more meaningful.

2019
Connection lost
status: offline
2019

The Quiet Season

Then came 2019, when our connection went quiet. Communication stopped, and we entered a period of silence that neither of us could simply overlook. It was an unexpected detour, a moment when our story seemed to lose its connection.

But even when a program goes offline, its story isn't necessarily over.

2020
Back online
git commit -m "refactor: stronger than before"
2020

Finding Our Way Back

In 2020, he returned, visiting my house after our long silence. I was still angry about the time we had spent apart, but he explained his reasons for being away, and we began communicating again. This time, things were different. He started expressing his feelings more openly, and our love gradually grew stronger and more solid.

Like refactoring a program, we were building on what already existed, strengthening our connection and giving our relationship a deeper meaning. We had found our way back to each other, and this time, the love was growing into something stronger.

Zamfara

2022

Miles Apart, Hearts Aligned

In 2022, another chapter began when he moved to Abuja while I remained in Zamfara. Our relationship became long-distance, and our connection lived through calls, chats, and messages. Though miles separated us, we continued nurturing what we had built, one conversation at a time.

We learned that love doesn't always require being in the same place; sometimes, it means continuing to choose each other despite the distance. Abuja and Zamfara were two different locations, but our hearts remained part of the same story.

24 May 2026

24 May 2026

The Introduction

Then came 2026, bringing us closer to the future we had been building toward. Early in the year, he told me he would be coming to see my parents for the formal introduction.

On 24 May 2026, that intention became reality. It was a beautiful milestone in our journey, bringing our families into a story that had begun ten years earlier with nothing more than a simple hello.

5 December 2026

Forever Begins

And now, on 5 December 2026, we prepare to begin a new chapter as husband and wife, insha Allah. Looking back, I never could have imagined that the man I noticed sitting outside his family's house would become the man I would choose to spend my life with.

From the squeaky bicycle and morning greetings to friendship, love, separation, reconciliation, and years of long-distance commitment, every phase has contributed to the story we share today.

Production Release
Not the final version

In software development, a production release marks the beginning of a project's life in the real world. But our wedding isn't the final version of our story; it is the beginning of a beautiful new phase. By Allah's grace, we are ready to keep learning, growing, and building together as husband and wife.
